The problem

India speaks many languages. Content should travel across all of them.

Creators reach audiences across languages, regions, and cultures. Reaching them takes more than translating dialogue: it takes context, regional expression, pronunciation, speaker identity, and the way people naturally communicate.

VYOMA treats localization as an end-to-end creative workflow, not a one-click translation.

The solution

More than dubbing. A complete creator-localization workflow.

A working prototype built around context, voice, and creator control.

01 / ADAPT

Context-aware localization

Guide regional wording, code-mixing, audience tone, and custom glossaries.

02 / VOICE

Intelligent voice generation

Use a consent-based voice clone or studio voices, including speaker assignment.

03 / DIRECT

Dialogue-level control

Review, edit, and regenerate individual lines with pronunciation guidance.

04 / VERIFY

DubBack checks

Re-transcribe generated speech and flag possible mismatches for human review.

05 / REMEMBER

Creator Kits

Reuse saved style profiles, pronunciation dictionaries, and audience personas.

06 / EXTEND

Beyond dubbing

Create audience-specific versions and draft spoken replies to comments.
