# VYOMA — LinkedIn launch post draft

**VYOMA (व्योम)**  
**AI-powered content localization**  
*One voice. Limitless reach.*

Creators have something worth sharing. Language shouldn’t limit who gets to hear it.

Introducing VYOMA: a content localization workspace built to help creators adapt short videos for Indian language audiences while keeping their voice and everyday code-mixing in the conversation.

With VYOMA, creators can:

- Localize a video into Indian languages with a cloned or studio voice.
- Guide regional wording, code-mixing, and pronunciation.
- Review and refine each spoken line, then fit it to the video’s timing.
- Export captions and compare audience engagement they enter themselves.

VYOMA is powered by sovereign Indian AI capabilities, with creator review at every step. The current prototype uses Gnani for transcription and speech, and Google Gemini for rewriting and semantic review.

We’re building toward a future where one creator’s message can travel further without losing the way they speak.

**One voice. Limitless reach.**
